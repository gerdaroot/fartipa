Sound effects of «Пиздец крутой байкер 3Д» (files sfx-*.caf in this folder)

Every source below is released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is required; it is given anyway.
The files here are edits of the originals: mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz 16-bit, trimmed,
sometimes slowed down, layered and loudness-matched. The fart sounds are the game authors' own
recordings (ios/PoopDraw/Sounds) and are not listed here.
